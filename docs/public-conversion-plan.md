**Decision, 18 November 2026.** Optimize for real, qualified WhatsApp inquiries. Keep **Premium → Classic → Basic** on every pricing page in Hebrew and Russian. The first release improves the conversation visitors start and the path to the correct package. Compact mobile comparison is a separate experiment.

**Reviewed against** local main at `5c7fa55` and the live public site. The browser review covered all seven pricing details in both languages, the seven Hebrew services, four Hebrew galleries, both Hebrew hubs, home, About, Contact, the couples campaign page, the articles index and all five Hebrew articles. **Why preserve the price order?** Research on price presentation found that descending order can shift choice toward higher-priced options when buyers associate price with quality. The studied choices were across brands. This does not establish a lift in inquiries or sales for one photographer selling different amounts of time and editing. On this site the entry price is already visible on several heroes and in some search snippets. Use Premium as the first *detailed offer*: explain what the extra time buys. Keep Classic easy to find. [Suk, Lee & Lichtenstein, Journal of Marketing Research (2012)](https://doi.org/10.1509/jmr.11.0309)

## 4. Measurement and release decision

1. **Before changing the UI**, capture the last complete 28 days and the preceding comparable 28 days. In GSC export queries/pages for both pricing path prefixes, device, clicks, impressions, CTR and position. In GA4 record sessions landing on each pricing URL, session channel and device, and count sessions with a WhatsApp `contact_click`. Separate Hebrew/Russian, organic/paid and package/header/floating. Use session counts for rates; one visitor may tap twice. The older `whatsapp_chat_start` and `click_to_call` series overlap the new event family. Never add them together. If current exports are unavailable, keep the baseline cells blank and record the date tracking starts.
2. **What counts as qualified:** a new inbound WhatsApp conversation about a session Yulia offers, where the prospect asks to discuss a date/availability or gives a relevant planning detail such as month, location or desired package. Deduplicate one conversation per prospective shoot; exclude existing-client logistics, repeats, unrelated offers and unsent link opens. A visitor may qualify before choosing a tier. Yulia can mark this in the workflow she already uses.
3. **Minimal weekly tally:** week, known source (website / other / unknown), known entry page or service, language if known, number of new chats, qualified chats, requested tier if known, and deposits/booked tier when known. Use aggregate counts for this review. Capture known source from the conversation or a brief natural question when convenient. A service/tier prefill alone cannot prove a Google visit or the precise landing URL. Do not collect customer identifiers in GA. Link taps, sent messages and deposits are three separate stages.
4. **Decision metrics:** primary business outcome = confirmed website-sourced qualified chats per comparable period. For the subset with credible pricing-page attribution, report qualified chats per 100 corresponding pricing landing sessions, with attribution coverage and unknowns alongside the number. If exact source or denominator cannot be aligned, report the two counts separately. Diagnostic = pricing landing sessions with a WhatsApp tap ÷ pricing landing sessions. Also report qualified share of known website chats, Classic/Premium share among known booked tiers, total deposits and booked value. Track mobile/desktop separately where volume permits. Increased taps with flat qualified chats do not justify wider rollout on their own.
5. **Release cadence:** ship tracking/context first, family and bilingual hub fixes next, then discovery/campaign fixes. Annotate release dates and unchanged comparison pages. Check technical behavior at release and within 48 hours, then review funnel and GSC weekly. Reassess after at least one comparable four-week window. Include raw numerators/denominators and changes in traffic source, promotions and availability. Treat sparse weeks or before/after observations as directional. A new ranking drop on one page calls for query/device and unchanged-page comparison before attributing a cause.
6. **Experiment decision:** only run D if traffic supports a usable comparison. Agree on a minimum worthwhile lift and acceptable change in booked value with Yulia using her real volume; size a two-arm test from that baseline. If a split is feasible use a stable assignment and record exposure without customer data. An attribution method capable of connecting qualified chats to a variant is needed before claiming a variant-level lead lift. If volume or attribution is insufficient, keep Premium first, watch visitors try the task in a few mobile usability sessions, and use optional comparison as a reversible pilot only. Hold or revert if qualified demand or value declines consistently, or either language has a broken path. Do not declare a winner from a handful of clicks.
